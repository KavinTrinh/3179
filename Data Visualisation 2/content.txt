Why first? Immediate visual impact, shows the big picture

Section Title: "The Global Carbon Landscape: Where Does Australia Stand?"

Narrative progression: "It's not just about being developed - it's about HOW we've developed"
Reveals: Australia's emission intensity relative to economic peers

Section Title: "Economic Growth vs Environmental Cost"

Chart 4: Multi-Line Chart - Emissions Over Time (Australia vs Selected Countries vs Global Average)

Why third? Shows whether we're improving or falling behind

Section Title: "Two Decades of Progress: Australia vs The World"


Chart 3: Choropleth Map - Australian States/Territories Emissions (2023)

Why fourth? Natural transition from global/temporal to local/spatial

Makes it personal: Viewers see their own state
Sets up: The sectoral analysis that follows

Section Title: "Inside Australia: A Nation Divided by Carbon"
